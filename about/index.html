About

20 years as the end user.

I'm a Clinical AI Product Manager. Before I built anything, I spent two decades in a nuclear medicine department — the person on the receiving end of every tool that was supposedly designed for me. Most of them weren't.

Now I build clinical tools shaped around how the work actually happens, not how a spec imagines it. I write this build log to keep myself honest about the gap between the two.

What I keep coming back to

  • Clinical AI doesn't fail in the model. It fails at the bedside.
  • Stop teaching doctors to prompt. Redesign the workflow instead.
  • The best clinical AI PM was never a PM.
